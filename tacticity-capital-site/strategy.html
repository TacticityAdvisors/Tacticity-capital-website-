OurStrategies

Tacticity Capital is active across the entire value chain of life sciences investments, from seed to later-stage, with a focus on healthcare and sustainability.

Tacticity Capital

The Capital Strategy is our flagship fund, investing as founding or lead investor in early-stage biopharma and medtech start-ups developing groundbreaking therapeutic technologies.

 Tacticity MD Start

The Tacticity MD Start Strategy is our in-house medtech accelerator focused on creating and launching early-stage medtech companies through hands-on company building.

 Tacticity Crossover

The Tacticity Crossover Strategy invests in growth-stage biotech and medtech companies that have clinically validated their technologies and are preparing for commercialization.

 Tacticity Industrial Biotech

Our Industrial Biotech Strategy invests in early-stage sustainable companies across the agriculture, chemicals, food and materials sectors.

 Tacticity Telethon

The Tacticity Telethon Strategy makes early-stage investments in leading Italian science for the benefit of patients around the world.

 Tacticity Digital Medicine

Our Digital Medicine Strategy backs early-stage techbio and healthtech start-ups at the intersection of biology, data, and computation.

 Tacticity Biovelocita

The Tacticity Biovelocita Strategy is our biotech accelerator, partnering with leading research organizations to create and build European biotech start-ups.
